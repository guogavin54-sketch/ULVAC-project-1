Our Business

Across a wide range of industries, ULVAC is a source of numerous products and technologies that meet the needs of our customers and the market. At present, ULVAC operates businesses in six areas.

Semiconductor and Electronic Device Production Equipment

Higher integration and higher performance at lower cost are demanded for next-generation semiconductors and other high-performance devices. Based on its development of super-miniaturization technologies for such semiconductors and other electronic devices, ULVAC delivers a stream of new technologies and products to the world that underpin the progress of society.

View Details

Display and Energy-Related Production Equipment

Leveraging vacuum technology cultivated over many years in a wide range of fields such as displays, smart energy, and life sciences, ULVAC provides creative and innovative coating and processing solutions for various materials including glass substrates, plastics, and films.

View Details

Industrial Equipment

ULVAC has been involved in this business sector since the emergence of vacuum technology in the 1950s. Leveraging the fundamental technology it has developed so far, ULVAC currently provides various solutions across wide-ranging industries, including the automobile, pharmaceutical, and food industries.

View Details

Components

As a comprehensive vacuum product manufacturer, ULVAC is also dedicated to the development of components. ULVAC pursues cutting-edge vacuum technology while providing a wide range of product lines, extending from vacuum pumps and measurement and analysis equipment, to components, such as power generators for various types of deposition equipment and vacuum valves.

View Details

Materials

ULVAC is a source of electric materials, such as sputtering targets, for diverse industries. In addition, in the high-performance materials sector, ULVAC is a source of integrated manufacturing and precision processing technologies for titanium, tantalum, zirconium, niobium, etc.
