About ULVAC

Top Message

Building a flourishing future by creating innovative solutions to deliver industrial and scientific advancement.

Setsuo Iwashita, President and CEO

Back in 1952, ULVAC was still a fledgling venture that had just been founded by young engineers aspiring to contribute to industrial and scientific advancement through the use of vacuum technologies with the financial support of six angel investors. While continuing to cherish the entrepreneurial spirit that it has embraced since its establishment, ULVAC remains committed to creating new value by pursuing cutting-edge innovation for our vacuum technologies.

Show More

Who We Are

ULVAC is a comprehensive vacuum equipment manufacturer that provides manufacturing equipment, components, analytical instruments, materials, and services based on its core vacuum technology. Under the basic corporate philosophy of "contributing to the development of industries and science," we pursue cutting-edge innovation and support people's lives across a wide range of industries including semiconductors, electronic devices, displays, automotive, and pharmaceuticals.

Basic Corporate Philosophy

ULVAC Group aims to contribute to the development of industries and science by comprehensively utilizing its vacuum and peripheral technologies through the mutual cooperation and collaboration of the Group companies.

Company History

When ULVAC was founded back in 1952, vacuum technology had not yet entered widespread use in Japan. As a trailblazer, we championed vacuum technology by introducing new technologies to the market and addressing the needs of customers in diverse industries.

1952 August

Relocated headquarters and Yokohama Plant to Chigasaki City.

1968 May

Japan Vacuum Engineering Co., Ltd. was founded (capital: 6 million yen) for the purpose of importing and selling various types of vacuum equipment.

1975 December

Established ULVAC North America Corp. (currently, ULVAC Technologies, Inc.), a subsidiary in North America, as an export base to the U.S.

1982 January

Established ULVAC TAIWAN Co., Ltd. (currently, ULVAC TAIWAN INC.) in Taipei City, Taiwan.

1987 February

Established ULVAC GmbH in West Germany to improve European services.

1995 September

Established ULVAC (NINGBO) Co., Ltd., a joint venture with Ningbo C.S.I. Power & Machinery Group Co., Ltd. in China.

2001 July

Changed company name to ULVAC, Inc.

2004 April

Joined the 1st Section of Tokyo Stock Exchange. Increased capital to 8.1 billion yen from 3.85 billion yen.

2022 April

Moved from the First Section of the Tokyo Stock Exchange to the Prime Market following the exchange's market restructuring.

2024 May

Established the Technology Center PYEONGTAEK in South Korea to accelerate product and technology development and strengthen collaboration and technical support.
